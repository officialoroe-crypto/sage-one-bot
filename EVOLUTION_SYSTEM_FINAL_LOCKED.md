## Three intensity states

Every Evolution has:
- **Low** — subtle particles, lower glow, gentle accents.
- **Mid** — more particles, stronger glow, clearer identity.
- **High** — maximum intensity, rich particles, evolution-ready state.

Therefore the system has **13 major Evolutions × 3 intensity states = 39 visual states**, while the product still presents the major Evolution as the user's primary rank.

Example:
**Evolution 01 — Bronze / State: High**

Transition:
**Current Evolution High → Evolution Transition → Next Evolution Low**
