# SAGE ONE — Brand Identity

## Canonical Logo

The image supplied by the owner on 2026-09-22 is the canonical SAGE ONE logo reference.

### Visual specification
- Primary mark: stylized metallic/energy **S**.
- The S is enclosed by a thin circular orbital ring.
- One luminous orb/coin travels on an orbit crossing around the S.
- Material language: polished chrome/silver with electric cyan, blue and violet illumination.
- Background: near-black/deep space.
- Lighting: cinematic bloom, star-like highlights and subtle atmospheric glow.
- Wordmark: **SAGE** in a futuristic geometric metallic treatment.
- Sub-mark: **ONE**, centered below SAGE with horizontal rules on both sides.
- Supporting line: **YOUR PERSONAL AI MENTOR & EXECUTION PARTNER**.
- The logo and orbital S mark are the canonical visual identity for SAGE ONE and sage.ai.

### Product rule
Use this logo/mark consistently across SAGE ONE product surfaces, app icon concepts, splash screen, navigation branding, Figma UI, documentation, marketing assets and future brand extensions. Do not replace it with a different SAGE symbol without explicit owner approval.

The UI should inherit the logo's premium cinematic language without becoming visually noisy: deep black foundations, restrained glass/metal surfaces, controlled cyan/blue/violet light accents, precise typography, orbital/trajectory motifs, and subtle motion.
