# Evolution Transition Storyboard

## Locked transition

**Current High → Evolution Transition → Next Low**

The transition sequence is:

1. Particles accelerate.
2. Energy concentrates toward the SAGE Core.
3. The screen/environment darkens slightly.
4. A controlled shockwave / burst occurs.
5. The old material identity breaks apart.
6. New material forms.
7. New particles appear.
8. The new rank crystallizes / resolves.
9. The interface settles into the next Evolution at Low intensity.

## Motion principles

- The transition changes controlled visual layers, not navigation structure.
- SAGE blue/black identity remains present throughout.
- The environment should not be replaced by a different world.
- Material changes should feel like an evolution around SAGE, not a replacement of SAGE.
- Motion must settle cleanly into the next Low state.
- Avoid permanent high-intensity motion after the transition completes.

## Timing model

- current evolution
- current intensity
- target evolution
- target intensity

and render the same shared transition system with material configuration supplied by data.
