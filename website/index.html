SAGE Spark (SS) and Spark Points (SP)

1 Spark = NPR 1

Spark (SS) is the money side. It is paid for verified work and spent on premium work. Spark Points (SP) are power points that achievements award: they have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Every account, including the owner's, starts at 0 with no hidden welcome balance.

  • Spark amounts are whole numbers. Converting rupees to Spark rounds down.
  • Spending Spark never lowers your Evolution.
  • Rewards are checked by the server and recorded once, so a one-time reward cannot be repeated.
Cash-out to rupees is not available yet

Paying out real rupees needs a payment provider, an owner switch and a legal review first. Until then, Spark stays inside SAGE.

How much SAGE may spend

SAGE earns first and the owner never funds it. Part of what it earns is locked as savings. One spend can only be a small share of lifetime earnings, and a hard ceiling applies at any size. SAGE can only propose a spend. You approve every one.

The owner's spend limits

  1. Bronze Low, Mid and High start at 1, 10 and 50 Spark. There is no lower start than 1.
  2. There are no Silver or Gold spend limits. Bronze High is the top level.
  3. The ceiling is 500 Spark. No single spend can be larger, at any size.
  4. The share of earnings per spend is 10% up to 100, 5% up to 1,000, 2% up to 10,000 and 1% above.

Where it starts, how it grows, where it ends

This follows the master blueprint in the project repository. The stages are in order. They are not dated.

  1. A. Make the private core realFix Google sign-in on the phone, move the backend to storage that survives restarts, verify the website, and walk every screen on a real device.
  2. B. SAGE earns its first SparkUse the sales and content workflow for the owner's first real customer. Every earning is a checked, one-time ledger entry.
  3. C. Spend limits go liveSAGE may propose spending inside its limits. One permissioned adapter at a time, each with a ceiling and a kill switch.
  4. D. Mentor depthVoice-first mentoring that remembers the owner, pushes back honestly and holds them accountable. The owner decides how strict it is.
  5. E. Share the serverOptional and last. Needs separate user data, a security and privacy review, payments law and support before anyone else uses it.
